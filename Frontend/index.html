Lifecycle

Four steps, each one a transaction.

Every bounty moves through the same on-chain path — nothing is settled off-chain or by admin override.

01

Create

An organization opens a bounty with a title and a vulnerability description, locking the ETH reward in createBounty().

Organization
02

Report

A researcher calls submitReport() with a hash of their findings — the evidence stays off-chain, the hash doesn't.

Researcher
03

Resolve

The organization calls resolveBounty() to accept or reject the report. Only they can close their own bounty.

Organization
04

Settle

Accepting a report releases the locked reward straight to the researcher's wallet in the same transaction.

Contract

Two sides

Built around who's actually using it.

Running a bounty program

Post a scope, back it with ETH, and only pay out when you're satisfied.

  • Reward is escrowed the moment you create the bounty
  • You decide whether a report is accepted
  • Every bounty you own is resolvable only by you

Hunting for bugs

Find something real, submit proof, get paid the moment it's accepted.

  • Browse every open bounty and its reward
  • Submit a report hash against any open bounty
  • Reward lands in your wallet the instant it's accepted
